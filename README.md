# Overview
Expanded Weaponry is a mod that enhances Minecraft combat in many ways. Its main focus is on providing more variety and choice in equipment.

I highly advise combining this mod with [Better Combat](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-combat)(if avaible for the selected version) as it offers cool animations that enhance gameplay and enable you to dual-wield weapons.


# New items
<details>
<summary>Melee weapons</summary>

### Hammer
![Crafting recipe of the hammer](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/157042bddac12b618d802a066b41e7693dda9b6a.png)

Heavy weapon capable of killing enemies with just 2 blows. Available in all materials.
- very high damage
- very slow attack speed
- two-handed

### Dagger
![Crafting recipe of the dagger](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/549742128954aaa1c02c0f6da65d06ab6121727f.png)

Lightweight and fast weapon that works the best at fighting multiple weaker opponents. Available in all materials.
- low damage
- very fast attack speed
- low range

### Twin Blade Sword
![Crafting recipe of the twin blade sword](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c8684f4f5703f5f7e5affcb9d290522c441e3bf2.png)

Fast and stylish weapon meant for fighting swarms of enemies. Available in all materials.
- medium damage
- fast attack speed
- two-handed

### Greatsword
![Crafting recipe of the greatsword](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/af7aba7659805d307bd83d96a93b9062ddb9434d.png)

Slow and heavy-hitting weapon with increased range. Great at keeping distance. Available in all materials.
- high damage
- slow attack speed
- far range
- two-handed

### Shields Material Tiers
![Crafting recipe of the shields](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/cc3e532c8b550e992b603cd18b0a88c7f92b0ec4.png)

Material tiers of shields. With every tier the shields block higher percentage of damage, have less axe disable cooldown and block more base damage.

- Min. **x** Damage Blocked

- **x**s of Disabled Cooldown
